# 12 — Expansion Path

Expansion follows proven Decision Case types, not disconnected features. Each increment reuses the evidence, semantics, lifecycle, governance, benchmark, and KPI contracts and adds scenario-specific fixtures before release.

## A. Deeper project control

Increase schedule/WBS depth, richer calendars and resource logic, broader progress methods, more integrations, portfolio-scale evidence operations, and improved prediction/calibration. Preserve the minimal-input path for lower digital maturity.

## B. Recovery decision intelligence

Compare bounded recovery scenarios, option cost/time/risk, approval/mobilization clocks, and outcome monitoring. VAI recommends options; humans approve changes, spend, and execution.

## C. Change-control intelligence

Link proposed changes to affected scope, schedule, cost, evidence, authority, and Decision Cases. Maintain strict separation among `PROPOSED`, `SCENARIO`, and `CURRENT_AUTHORIZED`.

## D. Procurement intelligence

Add long-lead items, submittals, approvals, manufacturing, logistics, required-on-site dates, substitutions, and downstream dependency impact as governed cases rather than a full procurement ERP.

## E. Commercial and claims intelligence

Add notice/entitlement evidence organization, event chronology, cost/time exposure, and decision support. Do not assign contractual liability or present analysis as legal/professional approval.

## F. Portfolio intelligence

Add cross-project priority, scarce-resource constraints, common supplier risks, comparative performance, and executive escalation while preserving tenant/project authority and contextual policy differences.

## G. Organizational learning

Use closed-case outcomes to calibrate source reliability, forecasts, confidence, thresholds, recovery effectiveness, and decision lead time. Learning produces proposed model/policy changes; it never silently rewrites prior cases or project baselines.

## Expansion gate

An increment proceeds only when its operator/decision is frozen, minimum evidence and authority boundary are defined, synthetic cases and prohibited outcomes exist, current architecture capacity is measured, pilot value is shown, and an explicit governance/product owner approves the expansion.
